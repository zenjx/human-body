Nervous System

What is it?
The nervous system receives information, processes it, and sends signals that help control the body. It helps us sense, move, think, learn, and respond.

Brain

Processes information and helps control thoughts, senses, movement, and memory.

Spinal Cord

Carries signals between the brain and much of the body and helps with reflexes.

Nerves

Carry messages between the central nervous system and other body parts.

Sensory Receptors

Detect changes such as touch, pressure, temperature, light, and sound.

❤️

Circulatory System

What is it?
It moves blood around the body, delivering oxygen and nutrients to cells and carrying away some waste products.

Heart

A muscular organ that pumps blood through blood vessels.

Blood

Transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and waste products.

Arteries and Veins

Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood toward it.

Capillaries

Tiny vessels where materials move between blood and body tissues.

🫁

Respiratory System

What is it?
It brings oxygen into the body and removes carbon dioxide when we breathe.

Lungs

Contain tiny air sacs where oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged.

Trachea

The main airway that carries air toward the lungs.

Bronchi

Branches from the trachea that carry air into each lung.

Diaphragm

A major breathing muscle that helps move air in and out.

🍎

Digestive System

What is it?
It breaks food into smaller substances that the body can absorb and use, then helps remove solid waste.

Stomach

Mixes food with digestive juices to help break it down.

Small Intestine

Most digestion and nutrient absorption happen here.

Large Intestine

Absorbs water and helps form solid waste.

Liver

Produces bile and performs many important processing functions.

🦴

Skeletal System

What is it?
It gives the body structure, protects organs, stores minerals, and works with muscles for movement.

Bones

Support the body, protect organs, and provide a framework for movement.

Bone Tissue

Living tissue that is strong, can repair itself, and stores minerals.

Joints

Places where bones meet and allow different kinds of movement.

Cartilage

Helps cushion joints and allows smooth movement between bones.

💪

Muscular System

What is it?
It helps the body move, maintain posture, move substances inside the body, and produce heat.

Skeletal Muscles

Attach to bones and allow movements like walking and lifting.

Muscle Fibers

Specialized cells that contract to create force and movement.

Muscle Tissues

Skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscle tissues have different jobs.

Tendons

Strong connective tissues that attach muscles to bones.
