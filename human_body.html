Nervous System

What is it?
The nervous system receives information, processes it, and sends signals that help control and coordinate the body. It helps us sense the world, move, think, learn, and respond.

Brain

The brain processes information and helps control thoughts, senses, movement, memory, and many body functions.

Spinal Cord

The spinal cord carries signals between the brain and much of the body and is involved in reflexes.

Nerves

Nerves carry messages between the central nervous system and other parts of the body.

Sensory Receptors

Sensory receptors detect changes such as touch, pressure, temperature, light, and sound.

❤️

Circulatory System

What is it?
The circulatory system moves blood throughout the body. It delivers oxygen and nutrients to cells and helps carry away waste products.

Heart

A muscular organ that pumps blood through the blood vessels.

Blood

A fluid tissue that transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and waste products.

Blood Vessels

Arteries, veins, and capillaries form pathways that carry blood around the body.

Capillaries

Tiny blood vessels where materials are exchanged between blood and nearby tissues.

🫁

Respiratory System

What is it?
The respiratory system brings oxygen into the body and removes carbon dioxide. It works closely with the circulatory system to deliver oxygen to cells.

Lungs

The lungs contain airways and tiny air sacs where oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged.

Trachea

The trachea is the main airway that carries air toward the bronchi and lungs.

Bronchi

The bronchi branch from the trachea and carry air into each lung.

Diaphragm

The diaphragm is a major breathing muscle that changes chest volume to help move air in and out.

🍎

Digestive System

What is it?
The digestive system breaks food into smaller substances that the body can absorb and use. It also moves waste out of the body.

Stomach

The stomach mixes food with digestive juices and helps break it down.

Small Intestine

Most digestion and nutrient absorption takes place in the small intestine.

Large Intestine

The large intestine absorbs water and helps form solid waste.

Liver

The liver produces bile and performs many important processing and storage functions.

🦴

Skeletal System

What is it?
The skeletal system gives the body structure, protects many organs, and works with muscles to make movement possible.

Bones

Bones support the body, protect organs, and provide a framework for movement.

Bone Tissue

Bone is living tissue that is strong enough to support the body while also changing and repairing itself.

Joints

Joints are places where bones meet and allow different types and ranges of movement.

Cartilage & Joint Structures

Cartilage helps cushion and support certain joints and allows smooth movement between bones.

💪

Muscular System

What is it?
The muscular system helps the body move, maintain posture, move substances inside the body, and produce heat.

Skeletal Muscles

Skeletal muscles attach to bones and allow voluntary movements such as walking and lifting.

Muscle Fibers

Muscle fibers are specialized cells that contract to create force and movement.

Muscle Tissues

The body has skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscle tissues, each adapted to different jobs.

Tendons

Tendons are strong connective tissues that attach muscles to bones and help transfer muscle force.
